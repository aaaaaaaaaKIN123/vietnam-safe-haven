# Data, attribution and release boundaries

The public source distribution excludes market datasets, derived model inputs,
fitted results, manuscript files, submission documents, literature PDFs and
downloaded third-party repositories. Acquire original data under the provider's
terms; the repository does not grant redistribution rights for those datasets.

`ML/`, `external/` and `src/models/WS time series/` are local reference materials
and are excluded from the release. Python/R dependencies retain their own licenses.
Method references remain in `src/models/model_method_map.yaml`.

The project currently has no selected software license. Public availability
does not by itself grant a broad reuse license. A future license should be chosen
by the author after confirming ownership of the distributed code.

The synthetic demonstration is generated by `tools/demo.py`; it contains no
observed financial records. Bond TRI variables used by the research are simulated
indices and must not be represented as official HNX/VBMA or vendor indices.

The release manifest records included files and their hashes. This is provenance
for a source snapshot, not a complete audit of originality or data licensing.
